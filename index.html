Teaching

Over the years I have taught about 17 different courses, mostly in computer systems: computer organization, operating systems, networks, and security. I am also passionate about interdisciplinary courses on computers and society, where I bring in applied ethics, moral philosophy, sci-fi literature, and international education. Three times I have taught ENGR 290 Engineering in a Global and Societal Context on the road: Brazil (2010), Italy (2015), and Italy and France (2024).

I prefer student-centric pedagogies: rather than lecture at students, I create opportunities for them to grapple directly with the material.
