Research

Simulation, robots, and the people around them

My research interests are modeling and simulation, human-robot interaction, and society and technology. Many of the papers below are co-authored with Bucknell undergraduates.

Areas

Modeling & simulation

Discrete-event and network simulation, experiment automation, and the credibility of simulation studies.

Human-robot interaction

Tools for Wizard-of-Oz studies and reproducible HRI experiments, including HRIStudio.

Society & technology

Ethics, privacy, and how computing and robots fit into everyday life.
