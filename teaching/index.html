Approach

Since joining Bucknell in 2003, I have taught 24 different courses and created or significantly revamped 14 of them. They include core and elective courses in computer science as well as interdisciplinary courses: Integrated Perspectives (UNIV 200), a Foundation Seminar (FOUN 098), and three international editions of ENGR 290.

My teaching philosophy is based on student-centric pedagogies: rather than lecture at students, I prefer to create opportunities for them to grapple directly with the material we study.

In my teaching, I seize every opportunity to explore the intersection of applied ethics, moral philosophy, sci-fi literature, and international education. Three of my courses have been taught “on the road” as ENGR 290 Engineering in a Global and Societal Context: in Brazil (2010), Italy (2015), and Italy and France (2024).
